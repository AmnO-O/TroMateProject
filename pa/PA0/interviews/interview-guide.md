# TroMate — User Interview Guide

*PA0 requires at least 5 interviews. Use this script so every interview covers the same ground and the evidence is comparable.*

## Before starting

- Ask for permission to **record** (audio/video). If declined, take written notes only.
- Keep it conversational — 10–15 minutes per person.
- Don't pitch TroMate at the start; first learn how they handle money today.
- Record the interviewee's background (see notes template).

## Section A — Background & context

1. What's your name, occupation, and how old are you?
2. What are your interests/hobbies?
3. Do you currently live in a shared room/apartment? With how many people?
4. Who pays the rent/landlord in your household? Who handles shared expenses?

## Section B — Current behavior & pain points

5. How do you track shared expenses today (app, Google Sheet, memory, notes)?
6. What do you find most annoying about splitting costs with roommates?
7. How does the end-of-month rent bill get calculated and settled? Walk me through the last time.
8. Have you ever had confusion, mistakes, or arguments over who owes what?
9. Do you ever feel awkward reminding a roommate to pay? How do you handle it?
10. Are there debts going in circles (A owes B, B owes C)? How do you untangle them?

## Section C — Reaction to the TroMate idea

11. How would you feel about logging expenses by just **typing/chatting** in a group chat instead of an app? (Show the example: *"bought detergent 120k, split for the whole room"*.)
12. Would you trust the bot to **read a photo of the rent bill** (OCR) and compute the split automatically?
13. Would a **VietQR code with the exact amount pre-filled** make paying easier? Any concerns?
14. Would the **meme/polite reminder** feature help or feel intrusive?
15. Which platform would you actually use: **Telegram, Zalo, or both**?
16. Which features matter most to you? (rank: expense logging, bill OCR, debt simplification, VietQR, meter scanning, chores, room fund, monthly report, deposit checklist)

## Section D — Adoption & closing

17. What would stop you from using something like this?
18. What would make you recommend it to a friend?
19. Would you pay for it? How much per month (in đồng)?
20. Is there anything else you'd want the bot to do?

*Thank the interviewee and confirm how to reach them for follow-up.*
